# Oversize nodes

## Intent

Seventeen of the map's forty-eight nodes are over the ~800-character bound in *Node Sizing (z9p)*, several at nearly double it. Each needs split-versus-keep settled on its own terms — a child node, a hard trim, or the user deciding the node earns its length.

# Unresolved at any stage

## Intent

Relax the Unresolved section so it can exist at any stage of a change, not only Approach. Its meaning stays "items needing the user", walked through one at a time. A non-empty Unresolved blocks approval of the stage it is tagged to. Held remains distinct as the passive spillway for on-topic material awaiting its part; its clearing mechanism is the opening of that part, not a user decision.

## Context

Agreed during the earlier `340-change-formality.md` discussion, which split into changes 010, 020 and 030 on 2026-09-29. The current map binds Unresolved to Approach and requires it empty before Approach approval; this change extends the concept to any stage, giving Intent-only (Trivial) and Conclude their own eligible slots.

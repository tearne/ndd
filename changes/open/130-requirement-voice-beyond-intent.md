# Requirement voice beyond Intent

## Intent

The guidance that a plan part is written in the voice of a requirement, such as "the application should…", should apply beyond the Intent to the other parts that state what a change must do or hold, such as *Bounds* and *Focus*, so none of them reads as a description of what already exists.

## Context

Raised as an aside on 2026-11-11 while drafting 001's Bounds: 3.5.0 gave the requirement voice to *Intent* alone, and the Bounds of past Vibe changes are written in the present tense.

# Duplication drift

## Purpose

Developing the map naturally leaves duplicated text across nodes: each addition restates the fact where it is added, as well as where it is owned. Change 070 is sweeping such fragments into single homes, which is important map maintenance. The question is why the duplication accumulates, whether other factors are at work too, and whether the method should prevent or catch it rather than relying on a periodic sweep. Raised as an aside during 070 (2026-10-20).

## Observations

- In 070 the pattern was a feature restating its effect in the node it touches: Versioning, Pause Keyword and Backlog Review each repeated what Startup Scan already reports, and Conclude, Versioning and Archiving each carried the changelog step.

## Open questions

- What other factors contribute, beyond additive edits restating facts at the point of addition?
- Should the method prevent duplication at edit time, or catch it in Map Review, as finding 1 of 070's review did?

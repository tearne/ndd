# Archiving simplification

## Intent

The Archiving and Startup Scan rules (rules 19 and 1) read as too complex, because each renders everything its nodes ask for at that moment. The Archiving rule, at over 800 characters, carries the changelog entry, Tidy, the move, companion folders, the lock, the Map Review offer and release steps; the Startup Scan node, over its size bound, keeps gaining report items. Simplify at the map first, so Archiving, the Startup Scan and what they delegate carry less, and the rules follow.

Raised during change 020 (2026-10-16).

Startup Scan added during change 090 (2026-10-18).

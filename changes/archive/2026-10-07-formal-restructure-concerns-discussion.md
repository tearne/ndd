# Formal restructure concerns

## Status and purpose

Discussion to park two concerns raised while checking the direction of change 050 (Change Style and Formal specification), for deeper consideration after Build. Neither blocks 050; both are about how the map hangs together once the new nodes exist and how it should shape behaviour.

## Vibe and drift

Of the four styles under Change Style, Vibe is the one where the preparation section (Bounds — agent-proposed discretion and review points) does the least structural work. Trivial has no preparation and is defined by no substantive ambiguity; Exploratory locks Focus around a stated question; Formal locks Specification as a target. Vibe agrees a discretion envelope and continues.

Open questions to consider later against the map:

- What does the Build node under Vibe actually govern? The Sync Rule and Build Lock are style-independent, but the day-to-day "am I still inside the agreed envelope" check has no obvious home. Does Bounds itself need to carry a mid-Build check node, or does Vibe's Build need its own commentary?
- How does Conclude read for Vibe? Formal Conclude verifies against Specification; Exploratory Conclude reports what the Focus resolved to. Vibe Conclude has no equivalent anchor — is that a gap in the Conclude node, or is Bounds the anchor Conclude cites?
- Does the reframe of Trivial (from "too fluid to plan" to "no substantive ambiguity") route borderline work into Vibe? If so, does Vibe's node text set expectations well enough to carry that load?

## Shape of the restructured Plan node

Under 050 the Plan node stops having a fixed sequence of children (Intent → Approach → Worklist) and gains a style-dependent shape (Intent → Bounds / Focus / Specification [+ Implementation Plan] / nothing). Open questions:

- Does the restructured Plan node read as one coherent shape parameterised by Change Style, or as four near-independent flows sharing only Intent and Build? The map's coherence, not the render's, is where this is decided.
- Do the new preparation nodes (Bounds, Focus, Specification, Implementation Plan) want their own sizing conventions on the Node Sizing node, or does one convention cover them?
- Does Follow-the-Plan (as a map concept, not a rule) still read the same way when the plan's shape is style-dependent? In particular, what does "return the change to planning" mean when the planning shape itself was chosen?


## Conclude

Six parked questions walked post-070/080/110. All six dropped on scrutiny: the map now carries clear answers (Vibe's trust-agent-judgement framing, parameterised Plan shape, distributed Node Sizing caps per preparation section, style-appropriate Follow-the-Plan via Formal Build's lock-kept rework). The concerns were raised in good faith during 050's Build; by this review they had already been absorbed. No candidates promoted.

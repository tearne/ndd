# Discussion approval noise

**Change Style:** Trivial

## Intent

Discussion documents currently attract the map-edit approval discipline — the agent surfaces content for approval, counts it, and settles it. Establish that Discussion updates are exempt from this discipline, keeping Discussion the low-friction record of unfinished thinking it was designed to be.

## Context

Parked mid-plan on 2026-09-30 after Intent was approved; Change Style not yet confirmed (Trivial was being proposed).

## Conclude

One map edit on Discussion Records making the Engagement Rule exemption explicit. Considered whether AGENT-RULES Rule 6 needed a parallel exemption clause and left it: Rule 6 is scoped to map edits by its own terms, and Discussion files aren't maps.
